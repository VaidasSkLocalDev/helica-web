Data we collect

  • Account data. When you sign in with Apple or Google we receive your name (when the provider shares it) and email address. We use no passwords of our own.
  • Client records. Coaches keep a record for each client: display name, email address, and invitation status.
  • Program and activity data. Exercise programs, homework and workout assignments with notes, and the completion history of exercises and workouts.
  • Posture scan results. A scan stores numeric measurements (for example shoulder or pelvis tilt in degrees) and the resulting finding labels. Scan photos are processed on the phone and are not uploaded — they exist only in the app's memory during a scan, and are saved to your device's photo library only when you explicitly choose to save them.
  • Email delivery log. When a coach emails a program PDF, we record to whom and when it was sent.
  • Invitation codes and confirmation links. Stored only as cryptographic hashes, never in readable form.
  • On your device only. App preferences, reminder settings, and onboarding drafts stay in local storage on your phone and are not transmitted to us.

What we do not collect

The apps contain no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs, and do not track you across other companies' apps or websites. We do not collect your location. We do not process payments and hold no payment details.

How we use your data

Solely to provide the service: operating coach–client program delivery, showing activity history, producing posture scan results, sending the emails described above, and keeping the service secure. Where the GDPR applies, our legal bases are performance of the service relationship, our legitimate interest in securing it, and your consent where we ask for it (for example notification, camera, and photo-library permissions). We do not sell or share personal data for advertising, and health-related measurements are never used for marketing.

Where data is stored and how it is secured

Backend data is hosted on Supabase in the European Union (Stockholm, Sweden). All traffic between the apps and the backend is encrypted (HTTPS). Access is enforced row-by-row on the server: coaches only reach the clients assigned to their practice role, and clients only their own records. Invitation codes and confirmation tokens are stored hashed.

Third parties that receive data

  • Supabase — hosts the backend database and authentication (EU region), processing all stored data above on our behalf.
  • Apple / Google — provide sign-in; they process your authentication under their own policies.
  • Resend — delivers our transactional emails (invitations, confirmation links, program PDFs) and receives the recipient address and email content for that purpose.
  • YouTube — exercise videos are embedded in privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com) and load only when you open a video; YouTube's own terms and privacy policy apply when you play one.

Some of these providers (for example Apple, Google, Resend, YouTube) may process data outside the European Economic Area under their own safeguards, such as EU standard contractual clauses.

Health-related data

Posture measurements and coach-assigned exercise programs are wellness information handled with care: they are visible only to you and your coaching practice, are never used for advertising, and are never sold. They are posture insights, not medical records, medical advice, or a diagnosis.

Your rights and controls

  • Delete your account — available in the Profile screen of both apps. It deletes your sign-in account, and removes the data linked to it. Records your coach created about you as their client (your program and history) remain part of the coaching practice's records; ask your coach or contact us to have them removed as well.
  • Sign out at any time.
  • Permissions — camera, photo-library ("add only") and notification access are all optional and requested through the standard iOS prompts; you can change them in iOS Settings.
  • Where the GDPR applies, you also have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in Lithuania, the State Data Protection Inspectorate). Contact [CONTACT EMAIL] to exercise them.
